Our story

A Denton family kitchen since 2016.

Casa Verde started with our grandmother's recipe box from Guadalajara and a tiny food truck off the square. Ten years later we're still grinding our salsas fresh every morning, pressing every tortilla by hand, and treating every guest like family.

Whether it's Taco Tuesday with friends, a birthday fiesta, or catering for 200 — pull up a chair. You're family here. 🇲🇽
